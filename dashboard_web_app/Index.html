Update grades from Google Classroom

Asks the school's grades computer to read the latest grades from Google Classroom and send them to this dashboard. Everyone who uses the dashboard then sees the new grades.
  • How long: about 10 minutes. You can close this box and keep working; the Update button shows the progress.
  • It works when: the grades computer is on, awake and online. The light below shows this.
  • It can't work when: that computer is off or asleep, or the grades were updated in the last 10 minutes.
  • Automatic update: every day at about 3:40 PM if the computer is on, so you often won't need this button.
  • It covers: all classes the school's High School and Junior High Classroom accounts can see, not your account.
  • It won't: change anything in Classroom, send any email, or change the grade reports for parents.
